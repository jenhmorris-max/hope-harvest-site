Product · Morphora

Every plant gets its own record.

Morphora is a collection tracker built the way plant people actually think. Not watering schedules and alarms, but a running history: when you last watered, when you last fed, where the plant came from, and how it has changed since. Each accession reads like its own page in a greenhouse ledger.

Days since, not days until

Plant people water when a plant needs it. Morphora logs each observation and shows you how long it has been, so your history builds into something you can actually learn from.

Sourcing and provenance

Record where each plant came from, what you paid, and which cutting came from which mother. Collections grow by trade and division, and the record follows.

Mycelium

An opt-in community feed where Morphora growers can share what is rooting, what is blooming, and what is for sale. A network, not a bulletin board.

Open Morphora Free to use right now. Works in any browser on your phone or desktop; app-store versions are on the way.

Follow along: @morphora_plants on Instagram and Morphora on Facebook.

Service · JHM Consulting

Senior HR judgment, sized for a small company.

JHM Consulting gives growing employers a practiced HR lead without a full-time salary. The work ranges from building an employee handbook from scratch to running clean, defensible I-9 verifications for remote hires. It is grounded in ten years of hands-on HR and operations across regulated industries.

An experienced HR partner on a retainer or per-project basis for companies that have outgrown doing it themselves.

I-9 verification and audits
